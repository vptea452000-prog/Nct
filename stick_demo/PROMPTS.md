# Bản thử phong cách người que — đầu Tập 5 "Cung tên" (25 giây)

## Lời dẫn (chủ kênh thu giọng)

> Sáng hôm ấy, Gầm xiên được con cá to nhất suối.
> Hắn giơ lên thật cao để khoe… và đó là sai lầm.
> Một con đại bàng sà xuống, cuỗm con cá ngay trên mũi giáo.
> Gầm phóng giáo đuổi theo — nhưng giáo nặng, chỉ bay được nửa đường.
> Còn con đại bàng thì ăn sáng rất thong thả.

Khoảng 60 từ, đọc thong thả vừa 25 giây. Mốc từng câu: 0:00 · 0:05 · 0:10 · 0:16 · 0:20.

---

## Prompt 1 — 0:00–0:10

10 seconds, vertical 9:16. Flat 2D stick-figure animation, clean black outlines, flat pastel colors, no gradients on characters. GAM: big round white head, messy spiky brown hair, thick dark-brown beard covering the lower half of his face, black dot eyes, heavy black eyebrows, thin black line limbs, short brown hide tunic shaped like a trapezoid, carries a long wooden spear with a grey stone tip. Setting: a shallow blue stream in front of soft green hills; on the far bank stands one tall tree with a single long bare branch high up.

0:00–0:03 — Wide side view. Gam stands knee-deep in the stream, spear raised overhead with both hands, tip angled down at the water. A dark fish shadow glides toward his shins. He holds still, eyes narrowing, then drives the spear straight down. A crown of white splash bursts up.
0:03–0:06 — Insert, low and close at water level. The stone spear tip rises out of the water toward the camera with a big blue-grey fish pinned on it, tail flapping, water drops streaming off.
0:06–0:10 — Low angle from behind Gam's back, sky filling the frame. He thrusts the spear straight up, the fish on top silhouetted against white clouds. Far up in the corner, a small black bird-shaped dot circles once, then stops dead mid-air. Hold on the dot.

Sound effects: gentle stream flow, soft whoosh of the spear, big water splash, water drips, cloth rustle as he lifts, faint distant wingbeats. Sound effects only. No voices, vocals, narration, dialogue, whispers, laughter, grunts, singing, crowd chatter, or music. All characters act silently. No lip-sync.

## Prompt 2 — 0:10–0:20

10 seconds, vertical 9:16. Flat 2D stick-figure animation, clean black outlines, flat pastel colors. GAM: big round white head, messy spiky brown hair, thick dark-brown beard covering the lower half of his face, black dot eyes, heavy black eyebrows, thin black line limbs, short brown hide tunic, long wooden spear with grey stone tip. EAGLE: chunky brown cartoon eagle, white head, bright yellow hooked beak, yellow talons. Setting: shallow blue stream, green hills, one tall tree on the far bank with a long branch high up.

0:00–0:03 — The eagle's point of view, diving straight down. Far below on the blue water, Gam is a tiny brown head with the fish held up on the spear. The water rushes closer with speed lines; at the last moment two yellow talons swing open into the edges of the frame.
0:03–0:06 — Wide side view. The eagle sweeps in from the left and plucks the fish off the spear tip without slowing. The empty spear springs up in Gam's hands. The eagle climbs across the stream and lands on the high branch of the tall tree, fish in its talons. Gam's head turns to follow, mouth falling open.
0:06–0:10 — Same wide view. Gam leans back and hurls the spear at the branch. It flies in a low arc, loses height halfway across, dips its stone tip and sticks into the mud in the middle of the stream, shaft quivering. Gam freezes with his throwing arm still out. Cut on the quivering shaft.

Sound effects: rising wind rush of the dive, sharp swoosh as the fish is snatched, heavy wingbeats, a thrown-spear whoosh, a wet thunk into mud, a wobbling wooden vibration. Sound effects only. No voices, vocals, narration, dialogue, whispers, laughter, grunts, singing, crowd chatter, or music. All characters act silently. No lip-sync.

## Prompt 3 — 0:20–0:25

5 seconds, vertical 9:16. Flat 2D stick-figure animation, clean black outlines, flat pastel colors. EAGLE: chunky brown cartoon eagle, white head, bright yellow hooked beak, yellow talons. GAM: big round white head, messy spiky brown hair, thick dark-brown beard, thin black line limbs, short brown hide tunic.

0:00–0:05 — Close shot along the high branch. The eagle sits large in frame with the fish under one foot and takes slow, unbothered bites. Far below and tiny at the bottom of the frame, Gam hops up and down at the water's edge, both fists in the air. Midway, the eagle pauses, glances down at him, blinks once, then lowers its head for another bite. Hold on the bite.

Sound effects: soft chewing crunches, a leaf rustle, one short distant eagle screech, faint stream. Sound effects only. No voices, vocals, narration, dialogue, whispers, laughter, grunts, singing, crowd chatter, or music. All characters act silently. No lip-sync.

### Kiểm tra khi tắt tiếng lời dẫn

Người xem vẫn theo được: Gầm xiên được cá → giơ khoe → đại bàng nhắm tới (chấm đen khựng lại) → góc nhìn của đại bàng lao xuống → cá bị cuỗm → phóng giáo hụt, cắm giữa suối → đại bàng ăn thong thả, liếc xuống rồi ăn tiếp. Mỗi câu lời dẫn trùng đúng cảnh đang chiếu; cú cuỗm cá không lộ trước câu "sai lầm".
